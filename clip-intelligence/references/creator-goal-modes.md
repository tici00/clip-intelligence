# Creator Goal Modes

Use the user's stated goal to prioritize relevant candidates. If the user does not specify a goal, use Balanced Discovery. Do not ask for a goal if a useful default analysis can proceed.

Goal modes influence Audience/Goal Fit and ordering. They do not change source facts, inflate scores, guarantee performance, or suppress a clearly stronger candidate without a reason.

## Balanced Discovery (default)

Aim for a useful, diverse shortlist across Comedy, Personality, Gameplay Progression, Curiosity, Story, Community, and Discovery where the source supports them. Do not force equal numbers per type.

## Growth / Discovery

Prioritize:
- Hooks understandable in the first practical seconds.
- Specific novelty, surprise, strong contrast, or a clear question.
- Moments with low context dependency.
- Clear payoffs or meaningful open loops.
- Packaging that accurately communicates the value to a new viewer.

Do not claim a candidate will go viral or guarantee reach.

## Community

Prioritize:
- Debates, choices, competitions, rankings, predictions, and audience-facing questions.
- Strong but authentic opinions that invite a response.
- Moments where viewers can meaningfully agree, disagree, vote, or share an experience.

Do not manufacture controversy or invent a question the creator did not imply. An editorial caption can invite discussion, but label it as a suggestion.

## Personality / Connection

Prioritize:
- Distinctive preferences, habits, reactions, values, personal stories, and creator-specific phrasing.
- Moments that help viewers understand the creator's personality.
- Emotional or relatable moments that retain their meaning outside the original stream.

Do not infer personal traits beyond what the source supports.

## Evergreen / Value

Prioritize:
- Useful tips, discoveries, explanations, comparisons, and repeatable lessons.
- Moments likely to remain useful beyond a short-lived news or game-update window.
- Self-contained explanations with enough context to be understood.

Flag time-sensitive facts and do not call a claim evergreen if its truth depends on a release, price, platform availability, or changing feature.

## User-defined goal

If the user names a different goal, summarize it briefly and apply it. If it conflicts with source evidence, explain the trade-off rather than forcing a result.

## Reporting

State the selected goal mode in Source & Scope. If a mode was inferred rather than explicitly selected, say it is the default. Explain goal fit briefly for each top candidate. Keep the primary shortlist ranked by overall usefulness for that goal, not by one metric alone.
